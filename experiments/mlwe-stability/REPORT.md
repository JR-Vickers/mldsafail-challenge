# MLWE Measurement-Stability Study

This is a descriptive, single-host, single-epoch observational cohort. It makes no statistical-significance or portability claim and cannot justify a scoring, tie-band, or environment change.

Cohort: 30 ranked runs and 13200 fresh executions.

- native: median 1.040483; range 1.034915–1.060627; CV 0.006651.
- adapter: median 1.073519; range 1.062730–1.111725; CV 0.015030.
- candidate: median 0.254224; range 0.252395–0.258657; CV 0.007901.

Adapter/native ratio median: 1.030576; candidate/adapter ratio median: 0.237030.
The frozen tie band is 1%; reported variation is descriptive rather than a decision rule.
