MLWE 0.5.0 · Methodology & safety

Measured progress on tiny module lattices.

Every submission uses repository-generated synthetic problems and independent candidate verification.

How results are produced

One immutable private epoch fixes 100 cases across five profile/eta cells and measures its primal-LLL reference. Each case receives one warmup and three measured worker executions. Workers run sequentially with no network, a read-only root, one CPU, a 2-GiB memory limit and a 60-second deadline. A trusted evaluator independently checks each returned candidate.

What the score means

The score is a reference-normalized complete-worker CPU ratio, minimized. Three successful measurements contribute their median CPU time, with a one-microsecond floor; failed cases receive the frozen 60-second penalty. Five cell geometric means have equal weight. The frozen reference ranked against itself scores exactly 1.0. Any invalid answer, including a warmup answer, makes the submission ineligible.

Comparison and uncertainty

Only results sharing benchmark version, evaluator fingerprint, hidden-suite version, worker class and epoch can rank together. Scores within 1% of a tie group's lowest score share a competitive rank. The frozen bootstrap produces a 95% uncertainty interval. Historical 0.4.0 abstract-operation scores belong to separate cohorts.

Private evidence

Seeds, nonce, raw candidates, measurements and source snapshots remain private to the evaluator. Public results contain only verified scores, aggregate diagnostics and non-secret cohort identities. Partial or incompatible evidence cannot rank. This retains the frozen local research timing contract.

Safety boundary

All executable experiments operate on deliberately tiny, locally generated instances. External keys, signatures and cryptographic targets are never accepted. This benchmark must not target deployed systems, forge ML-DSA signatures or become a utility for attacking practical parameters.
